General & Verification

Which cities, tehsils, and union councils do you cover for doorstep visits?

Facilitator Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) provides doorstep document visits across Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho, and Jabar. Online consultation and digital submission assistance are also available for applicants across all districts of Punjab via WhatsApp at 0345-5583530.

How long does an official doorstep documentation visit take?

A standard doorstep visit typically takes 15 to 25 minutes. During this time, Facilitator Code 90807 verifies your original CNIC, captures live biometrics or photographs, reviews supporting documents, and submits your application directly on the official Government of Punjab portal (dastak.punjab.gov.pk).

How can I verify my application status and government fee payment?

Every application processed under Facilitator Code 90807 generates an official Government of Punjab tracking ID and a 17-digit PSID payment challan. You can pay fees directly through JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or any bank app and track live progress on the Dastak Citizen App.

How do I book an official doorstep document facilitation appointment?

You can book a doorstep visit by downloading the Maryam Ki Dastak Citizen App, selecting your required service, and entering Facilitator Code 90807, or by messaging Muhammad Usman directly on WhatsApp at 0345-5583530 for instant document review and scheduling.

How do I verify that Muhammad Usman is an official Punjab Dastak Facilitator?

Check the official Dastak portal or call 1202 to verify the program and confirm Facilitator Code 90807. Use the official application reference to track your request and keep any payment receipt.

How can I verify my application and payment when using Facilitator Code 90807?

You can verify Facilitator Code 90807 through the official Dastak channels and ask for the application reference and official payment receipt. The issuing department reviews and approves each request.

Is this a government website?

No. It is an independent facilitation service run by a registered facilitator of the Maryam Ki Dastak program. Always verify details on the official Dastak portal or helpline 1202.

How do I book a service?

Book in the official Dastak Citizen App, choose Doorstep Service and enter Facilitator Code 90807. You can also message me on WhatsApp, call me on 0345-5583530, or call the helpline 1202.

How do I pay?

Fees are generated automatically by the official system. Payment options shown in the Dastak app include PSID (payable through any banking app or mobile wallet), IBFT, JazzCash, Easypaisa and cash on delivery. Available options may vary by service.

Do you take money for government fees?

No. Government fees are generated by the official system and paid through its payment options. I do not receive or hold them.

Can I get a refund?

Fees are generated by the official Dastak system and paid through its payment options. Muhammad Usman does not receive or hold these government fees and cannot refund, reverse or cancel them. Any refund or cancellation is decided by the relevant government department under the official system's rules. Please check the official Dastak portal or helpline 1202.

Which areas do you serve?

Doorstep help in Gujar Khan, Kallar Syedan and surrounding areas, where available. Inquiries accepted 24 hours.

How do I know you are really registered?

Check the official Dastak portal or call 1202, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 when you book in the app. Proof of registration is available on request via WhatsApp.

Driving License Services

What documents are required for a new Punjab learner driving permit?

To apply for a new Punjab Learner Driving Permit, you only need your valid original CNIC and an active mobile number registered on your name. Facilitator Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) completes the online registration and generates your official PSID fee challan from home.

Can I renew a Punjab driving license that expired more than 5 years ago?

Licenses expired for less than 5 years can be renewed easily online or at your doorstep via Code 90807. However, under Punjab Traffic Police rules, driving licenses expired for 5 years or more require a physical visit to a Traffic Licensing Center or Police Khidmat Markaz for re-testing.

Can I apply for an International Driving Permit (IDP) at my doorstep?

Yes. If you hold a valid regular Punjab driving license and a valid Pakistani passport with a visa, Facilitator Code 90807 can process your International Driving Permit (IDP) online from Gujar Khan or Rawalpindi—saving you from standing in long traffic office queues.

What is the process for replacing a lost or duplicate Punjab driving license?

To get a duplicate Punjab driving license, you first need an official Police Lost Report (DD entry). Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) assists you with both the online Lost Report and your Duplicate Driving License application through official Punjab Police portals.

How can I renew my expired Punjab driving license from home?

Ask about the available renewal service for your Punjab license category. The facilitator can help check your documents and explain any medical or identity requirements before submission. The licensing authority handles approval and delivery.

What documents are required for a new learner driving permit?

Keep your original CNIC, an active mobile number and the official fee ready. Check the Dastak Citizen App for the documents and eligibility rules for your license category.

What is the process for replacing a lost or duplicate driving license?

The facilitator can help check your licensing record and explain how to request a duplicate through Punjab DLIMS. Ask whether a lost-document report is required for your case.

Do you assist with driving license renewals for Islamabad, AJK, or Sindh?

While our core on-ground doorstep coverage operates under Maryam Ki Dastak across Punjab, we also provide specialized digital procedural guidance, file compliance checks, and online appointment booking assistance for driving license renewals, duplicate licenses, and learner permits across Islamabad Capital Territory (ICT), Azad Jammu & Kashmir (AJK), and Sindh licensing authorities.

Excise & Vehicle Services

What is the official fee structure for bike and vehicle ownership transfers in Punjab?

Government transfer fees depend on vehicle engine capacity (CC), filer/non-filer status, and registration year, plus a transparent PKR 950 Dastak facilitation fee. You can calculate exact costs using our online fee calculator on `www.dastak90807.com` or message 0345-5583530 for a free estimate.

How can I clear pending annual motor vehicle token taxes online?

Avoid late penalties by sharing your vehicle registration number on WhatsApp (0345-5583530). Facilitator Code 90807 checks your pending Punjab Excise token tax dues, generates an official PSID challan, and updates your e-payment record immediately after payment.

How do you facilitate new motorcycle or car registration with Punjab Excise?

For new bike or car registration in Punjab, Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) verifies your sales invoice, sale certificate, and owner CNIC, enters the data into the Punjab Excise portal, generates the official PSID fee slip, and guides you through biometric verification.

Is biometric verification mandatory for both buyer and seller during vehicle transfer?

Yes, under Punjab Excise & Taxation rules, live biometric verification is mandatory for both the seller and the buyer. Facilitator Code 90807 guides you through the e-Pay/Excise biometric process in Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi so your smart card transfers smoothly.

How do you facilitate vehicle and motorcycle ownership transfers in Gujar Khan?

Vehicle ownership transfers in Punjab require official paperwork compliance and buyer/seller verification. Under Maryam Ki Dastak (Code: 90807), we assist you by reviewing your registration documents, clearing outstanding liabilities, generating official payment challans (PSID), and submitting the transfer through the Excise & Taxation portal. We also guide both the buyer and seller through the mandatory biometric verification procedure so the transaction completes smoothly.

What is the official fee structure for bike and vehicle ownership transfers?

For a car or motorcycle transfer, budget PKR 950 for doorstep facilitation plus the official Punjab Excise challan. The total can vary by vehicle and any outstanding dues. Confirm the final amount in the official Dastak app before payment.

Punjab Police Services

What is General Police Verification, and why is it needed?

A General Police Verification certificate confirms that an applicant has no criminal record in Punjab. It is required for local employment, bank jobs, security clearances, and official memberships. Book through Code 90807 to get your verification processed without visiting a police station.

How does legal Tenant Registration work at your doorstep?

Under Punjab Tenancy laws, landlords and tenants must register rental agreements with the local police station. Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) handles online Tenant Registration by verifying CNIC copies and tenancy details from your doorstep in Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi.

Can I file an online Lost Report for a missing CNIC, passport, or documents?

Yes. If you lose your CNIC, passport, educational certificates, or vehicle smart card, Facilitator Code 90807 submits an official Punjab Police Lost Report online and delivers the verified digital copy needed for duplicate document issuance.

How long does it take to receive a Police Character Certificate in Punjab?

Once your CNIC, passport copy, and stay details are submitted through Maryam Ki Dastak (Code: 90807), the Police Character Certificate is typically verified and issued within 3 to 5 working days directly through official Punjab Police channels.

How do I obtain a Police Character Certificate for foreign visas or immigration?

Ask the facilitator to review the required documents for a Police Character Certificate, including your CNIC, passport details and photograph where applicable. The issuing authority handles verification and approval.

Revenue, Domicile & Civil Records

Can you help obtain Fard Malkiat (Mutation Copy) and E-Stamps in Punjab?

Yes. Facilitator Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) assists residents in Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi district with issuing official E-Stamp papers (for affidavits, agreements, and property registries) and obtaining verified Fard Malkiat copies through Punjab Land Records Authority (PLRA) channels.

What documents are needed to apply for a Punjab Domicile Certificate?

To apply for a Punjab Domicile Certificate in Rawalpindi district, you need your CNIC (or Form-B), father’s CNIC, educational certificates, and utility bill or voter list proof. Code 90807 prepares your complete file and tracks approval from home.

Do you facilitate Birth, Death, Marriage, and Divorce Certificates?

Yes. Through Maryam Ki Dastak (Code: 90807), we assist citizens across Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi in preparing and submitting applications for official Union Council Birth, Death, Marriage (Nikah), and Divorce Certificates without repeated office visits.

Can you assist with Wildlife Permits for partridges and quails in Punjab?

Yes. Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) facilitates official Punjab Wildlife possession and hunting permits for partridges (Teetar) and quails (Batair) through the Dastak portal (dastak.punjab.gov.pk), ensuring full legal compliance from your home.

How do I apply for a Punjab Domicile Certificate from home?

The facilitator can help review your CNIC or B-Form and proof of residence. Ask which documents apply to your case before submitting through the official channel.

Can you help obtain Fard Malkiat and e-stamps?

Ask about help obtaining Fard Malkiat through the Punjab Land Records Authority or generating an official e-stamp. The records and requirements depend on the property and transaction.

What happens to my documents?

Customer documents are deleted as soon as the application is complete and delivered. I never ask for passwords or OTPs.
