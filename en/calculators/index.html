Calculator FAQs

Are the fee calculations accurate?

They are estimates based on the details you enter. The official Punjab Government PSID decides the final amount, so always check the PSID before you pay.

How is the PKR 950 doorstep fee applied?

The PKR 950 doorstep facilitation charge is shown separately from government charges when it applies.

What if my driving license expired more than 5 years ago?

You can review an estimate, but a license that expired five years ago or more may need a visit to a Traffic Licensing Center or a Police Khidmat Markaz.

How do I pay the official PSID challan?

Use the PSID generated by the official Punjab system and pay through one of its supported bank or mobile-wallet channels.

Why does the car calculator ask for filer status?

Token tax and transfer charges for cars can depend on whether the owner is an active taxpayer on the FBR list, so the car calculator asks for that status.
