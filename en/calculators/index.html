Calculator FAQs

Are these Punjab driving license and vehicle transfer fee calculations accurate for 2026–27?

They are estimates based on the current inputs. The official Punjab Government PSID determines the final payable amount.

How is the PKR 950 Dastak doorstep facilitation fee applied?

The PKR 950 doorstep facilitation charge is shown separately from government charges when applicable.

Can I calculate renewal fees for a Punjab driving license expired for more than 5 years?

You can review an estimate, but a license expired for five years or more may require a visit to a Traffic Licensing Center or Police Khidmat Markaz.

How do I pay the official Punjab Government PSID challan after calculating my fee?

Use the PSID generated by the official system and pay through one of its supported banking or mobile-wallet channels.
