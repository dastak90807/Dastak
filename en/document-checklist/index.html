FAQs

Do I need every document on the list?

Documents marked "if applicable" depend on your case. The others are normally needed. Requirements can change, so confirm the latest list on the relevant official Punjab portal before you submit.

Is this checklist from the government?

No. It is a preparation tool by Muhammad Usman, an independent Registered Facilitator (Code 90807). It is not a government service and it does not submit anything for you.

Can you check my documents on WhatsApp?

Yes. Tick what you have and tap "Send my checklist on WhatsApp". I will tell you what is missing. Please never send passwords or OTPs.
