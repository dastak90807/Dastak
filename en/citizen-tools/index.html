Portals FAQs

Is this page an official government site?

No. This is an independent links page by Muhammad Usman, Registered Facilitator (Code 90807). Each link opens the department's own website. The official Maryam Ki Dastak portal is dastak.punjab.gov.pk.

Why does a portal link not open?

External websites can redirect, ask you to sign in, or be down for a while. Try again later, or start from the department's own homepage.

Which portals do I use to track my driving license?

First check printing and delivery on the DLIMS tracking page. Then use the Pakistan Post tracking page with the tracking ID that DLIMS gives you.
