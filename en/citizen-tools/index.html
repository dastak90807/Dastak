YOUR LINKS TO OFFICIAL SERVICES

Official Punjab Verification & Tracking Portals

Track DLIMS driving licenses, verify MTMIS vehicle records, check Safe City e-challans, and validate police, domicile, or e-stamp certificates on official Punjab portals. For doorstep help in Gujar Khan or Rawalpindi, contact Facilitator Code 90807.

Driving License Tools (DLIMS Punjab)

  • Track Driving License Printing & Delivery ↗Check card printing and delivery with the details requested by DLIMS.
  • Track License Card Delivery (Pakistan Post UMS) ↗Open the Pakistan Post EMTTS portal and enter the tracking ID received from DLIMS to check postal dispatch status.
  • Verify Driving License Authenticity ↗Verify a Punjab driving license through the official system.
  • Download Official e-License ↗Access your digital Punjab driving license when available.

Excise, Vehicle & e-Challan Tools

  • Online Vehicle Verification (MTMIS) ↗Check Punjab vehicle registration details.
  • Vehicle e-Registration Card (external) ↗Check this service with Punjab Excise before entering vehicle or identity details.
  • Check Safe City e-Challan ↗Check pending Punjab Safe City traffic challans.
  • Excise Attractive Number e-Auction ↗View the official registration-number auction service.

Police, Revenue & Civil Verification

  • Verify Police Certificate ↗Check an issued Punjab Police certificate.
  • Police Khidmat Markaz Citizen Portal ↗Find lost report, tenant registration, and verification services.
  • Punjab Domicile Verification Portal ↗Check domicile services and status.
  • Punjab E-Stamp Portal ↗Generate or verify an e-stamp through the official PLRA system.
  • PLRA Land Records ↗Find Fard Malkiat and digital land services.

Official Dastak & PSID Payments

  • Maryam Ki Dastak Citizen Portal ↗Access the official Punjab doorstep-services portal.
  • e-Pay Punjab ↗Pay official Punjab Government PSID challans.

External websites may redirect, require sign-in or be temporarily unavailable. Follow their current instructions. If a direct tool has moved, start at its department’s official homepage.
