Other Services FAQs

Can you help with every service on the official Dastak portal?

Not always. I handle many services, but the full list is on the official Dastak portal. Message me the exact service name and I will tell you honestly if I can help.

Can I book a service that is not on this website?

If the service is in the Dastak app, you can book it there as Self or On Behalf, with Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box. Ask me first if you are not sure which option to choose.

Do you visit my home for every service?

Doorstep visits are available in Gujar Khan, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho, Jabbar and nearby areas. Many services can also be done online for anywhere in Punjab. I confirm this before you book.

How much will another Dastak service cost?

The government fee is generated by the official system for each service. My doorstep fee is shown separately when it applies. Ask me before you book so you know the total.

How do I book Other Punjab Dastak Services?

  1. Book in the official Dastak Citizen App: choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box.
  2. Or message me on WhatsApp.
  3. Or call me on 0345-5583530. You can also call the Dastak helpline 1202 to book or for support.
