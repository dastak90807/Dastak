How booking works

The steps are the same for every service. I use the app’s own option names.

Questions

Services FAQs

How do I book a service with Facilitator Code 90807?

Open the Maryam Ki Dastak app and choose your service. Choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box. You can also message me on WhatsApp and I will guide you.

What is the difference between Doorstep Delivery and Self Service?

In the app, Doorstep Delivery means a Muavin (delivery assistant) is assigned to transfer your documents. Self Service means you collect the documents yourself. The Facilitator Code box is not available on the Self Service path, so choose Doorstep Delivery to book with Code 90807. After that, the app asks if the request is by Self or On Behalf.

Are the fees shown before I pay?

Yes. The app shows a payment breakdown before you confirm, with the service fee and the facilitator fee shown separately. Platform charges apply. I explain the breakdown on WhatsApp if you ask.

Where do you offer doorstep visits?

Doorstep visits are available where possible in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho and Jabbar. Online help is available across Punjab.

Is Dastak 90807 a government office?

No. It is an independent facilitation service run by a Registered Maryam Ki Dastak Facilitator (Code 90807). Applications go through the official Punjab Dastak system.
