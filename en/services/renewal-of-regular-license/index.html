Quick answer

You can apply for Renewal of Regular License from home, anywhere in Punjab. It is a Dastak service (DLIMS Services (Driving License)). Doorstep visits are available in Gujar Khan. Book in the Dastak app (see How to book below), or reach out via our WhatsApp support.

What documents do I need for Renewal of Regular License?

  • CNIC of applicant
  • Recent live photograph with white background (upper shoulders)
  • Old driving license
  • FIR (only if the original license was lost)
  • Medical certificate (only required if age is above 50)

How you receive it: Sent by courier.

No fixed delivery time. It depends on the department. You can track your driving licence status online, or get your digital licence from the official DLIMS e-License page.

How do I apply for Renewal of Regular License step by step?

  1. Open the official Dastak Citizen App and choose this service.
  2. Choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter our facilitator code.
  3. Keep your documents ready as listed above.
  4. Pay the fee generated by the official system.

How long does it take, and what can cause delays?

The time is not fixed; it depends on the department. I share status updates with you on WhatsApp. The renewed licence is sent by courier, and delivery time may vary. The department must approve the application. The Dastak app shows the current details when you book.

  • Your CNIC must be valid and the copy clear.
  • The photograph must be live, on a white background (upper shoulders).
  • A medical certificate is needed if you are above 50 years.
  • If the old licence was lost, an FIR is needed as well.
  • Missing documents can cause delay or rejection.

The licence can be renewed for 1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 years. The fee depends on the licence category and the years you choose. Use the driving licence fee calculator for an estimate.

Renewal or duplicate licence: which one do I need?

If you still have your licence and it is expiring or expired, apply for renewal. If it is lost, stolen or damaged, the usual route is a duplicate driving licence. The facilitator guide says that if a licence is lost or stolen in its final year before expiry, you should apply for renewal instead of a duplicate. For a full walk-through, read our Punjab driving licence renewal guide.

How long does driving licence renewal take in Punjab?

The time is not fixed; it depends on the department. The renewed licence is sent by courier. The department must approve the application, so the actual time can vary.

Can I renew my driving licence if it was lost?

Yes, but an FIR for the lost licence is needed with the other documents. If the licence was lost in its final year before expiry, the guide says to apply for renewal, not a duplicate. Check the Dastak app for the current requirements.
