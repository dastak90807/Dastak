Maryam Ki Dastak FAQs

What is Maryam Ki Dastak?

It is a Government of Punjab program that delivers many citizen services to your doorstep through the Dastak app. I am a registered facilitator under the program (Code 90807).

How do I get government documents at my doorstep in Punjab?

Book the service in the Dastak Citizen App, choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807. You can also message me on WhatsApp and I will guide you step by step.

Do I still need to visit a government office?

For many services, no. You book in the Dastak app or through me, and the document is delivered or completed for you. Some services may still need a visit, and I will tell you before you book.

How do I know the fee is correct?

The fee is generated by the official system. You can also try our free calculators for an estimate, then check the PSID before you pay.

Is this a government helpline?

No. This is an independent facilitation website. Official help is also available on the Dastak portal and helpline 1202.

How do I book General Inquiry and Guide?

  1. Book in the official Dastak Citizen App: choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box.
  2. Or message me on WhatsApp.
  3. Or call me on 0345-5583530. You can also call the Dastak helpline 1202 to book or for support.
