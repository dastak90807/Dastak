Questions

Contact FAQs

What is the fastest way to contact you?

WhatsApp on 0345-5583530 is the fastest. You can also call, or email support@dastak90807.com. Inquiries are accepted 24 hours a day.

What should I send in my first message?

Tell me the service you need, your area, and whether you want doorstep help in Gujar Khan or online help. I will reply with the documents you need and the payment breakdown. Please do not send your CNIC number, passwords, OTPs or bank details in a message.

Do you visit my home or office?

Doorstep visits are available where possible in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho and Jabbar. Online help is available across Punjab.

How can I verify that Code 90807 is real?

Open the Maryam Ki Dastak app, choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box. You can also check the official portal at dastak.punjab.gov.pk or call Helpline 1202.

Is Dastak 90807 a government office?

No. It is an independent facilitation service run by a Registered Maryam Ki Dastak Facilitator. Applications are submitted through the official Punjab Dastak system, and fees are paid through the official PSID.

Dastak 90807 is an independent facilitation service, not a government office. Inquiries are accepted 24 hours. Official department working hours and processing times are different. Official portal: dastak.punjab.gov.pk, helpline 1202.
