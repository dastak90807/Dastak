About and how to verify me

I am Muhammad Usman, a Registered Facilitator under the Government of Punjab's Maryam Ki Dastak program. My Facilitator Code is 90807. I work from Gujar Khan and help citizens in Rawalpindi and across Punjab. Doorstep help in Gujar Khan, Kallar Syedan and surrounding areas, where available. Inquiries accepted 24 hours.

From Sandal Road, Ward No. 14, Gujar Khan, Punjab 47850, I assist with driving licenses, police certificates and reports, vehicle registration and transfer, token tax, domicile certificates, and e-stamps. Doorstep visits cover Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, and Bewal where available; online inquiries are welcome across Punjab.

Why people choose this service

  • Applications go through official Punjab Government channels under Facilitator Code 90807.
  • Doorstep document checks and submission help reduce office visits.
  • Government fees and the facilitation charge are shown separately, with WhatsApp inquiries accepted 24 hours a day.

How to check me

  1. Visit the official Dastak portal or call the helpline 1202.
  2. In the Dastak Citizen App, choose Doorstep Service and enter Facilitator Code 90807.
  3. Proof of my registration is available on request via WhatsApp. I do not publish my documents or ID online.
