My story

Why I became a facilitator

Have you ever taken a day off from work, traveled to another city, and stood outside a government office only to hear, “The system is down” or “Come back tomorrow”?

I have.

Before I ever became a facilitator, I was just an ordinary citizen trying to get my motorcycle registered. Leaving my hometown, wasting precious time, and facing closed counters and absent operators made me feel the silent helplessness that thousands of citizens endure every single day. I promised myself that if I ever got the chance to spare others from this struggle, I would take it.

That chance arrived with the Government of Punjab’s Maryam Ki Dastak initiative. Driven by a pure intention to serve and guide my community, I applied, and by the grace of Allah, I was selected as Registered Facilitator #90807.

Since that day, I have dedicated myself to turning frustration into relief:

  • 700+ Services Completed: Helping families across Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, and Punjab get their essential documents from the comfort of their homes.
  • 4.9 Google Rating: Earned through honest guidance, zero hidden hassles, and treating every client’s work like my own.Google rating from 78 reviews, as of 24 November 2026.
  • Doorstep Convenience: From vehicle registrations and transfers to driving licenses, domiciles, and police certificates, no long queues, no wasted trips.Doorstep visits in Gujar Khan and nearby areas where available. Online across Punjab.

My true reward isn’t just a completed application; it is the peace of mind on a citizen’s face when their work gets done without them ever having to step out of their home.

Let me handle the hassle, so you can focus on what matters most.

Questions

About me and verification: FAQs

Is Dastak 90807 a government office?

No. Dastak 90807 is an independent facilitation service run by Muhammad Usman, a Registered Maryam Ki Dastak Facilitator (Code 90807). Applications are submitted through the official Punjab Dastak system, and fees are paid through the official PSID.

How do I verify Facilitator Code 90807?

Open the Maryam Ki Dastak app, choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807 in the Facilitator Code box. You can also check the official portal at dastak.punjab.gov.pk or call Helpline 1202. Proof of registration is available on request via WhatsApp.

Do you only work in Gujar Khan?

Doorstep visits are available in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho and Jabbar where available. Online help is available across Punjab.

Are government fees and your facilitation charge shown separately?

Yes. Government fees and the facilitation charge are shown separately, and I explain the payment breakdown before you pay.

How can I contact you?

WhatsApp or call 0345-5583530, or email support@dastak90807.com. Inquiries are accepted 24 hours a day. Office: Sandal Road, Ward No. 14, Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Punjab.
