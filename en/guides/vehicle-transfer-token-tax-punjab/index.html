Complete Guide to Vehicle & Motorcycle Ownership Transfer and Token Tax in Punjab (2026)

By Muhammad Usman · Facilitator Code 90807 · Updated 21 November 2026

Quick answer

To transfer ownership of a car or motorcycle in Punjab, both the seller and buyer must provide original registration documents, valid CNICs, and complete mandatory live biometric verification. Through Maryam Ki Dastak (Registered Facilitator Code: 90807), Muhammad Usman facilitates complete file verification, official Punjab Excise PSID challan generation, biometric guidance, and online token tax clearance at your doorstep in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, and across Punjab.

1. Required Documents for Vehicle & Bike Ownership Transfer in Punjab

Before starting your transfer request with Punjab Excise & Taxation, ensure you have the following documents ready:

  • Original Registration Book or Smart Card of the vehicle or motorcycle.
  • Valid Original CNICs of both the registered owner (seller) and the purchaser (buyer).
  • Signed Transfer Deed (TO Form) and valid sale receipt/stamp paper.
  • Cleared Annual Token Tax up to the current financial year (pending token taxes must be cleared before transfer).
  • Active Mobile Number registered in the buyer's name for official Excise SMS alerts.

2. Step-by-Step Ownership Transfer Process (Doorstep & Online)

  • Step 1 – Document & Dues Check: Share clear photos of your vehicle smart card/book and CNICs with Facilitator Muhammad Usman (Code: 90807) on WhatsApp (0345-5583530) to check for any pending token tax or e-challans.
  • Step 2 – Official PSID Challan Generation: We submit the transfer application on the official Punjab Excise portal and generate your 17-digit PSID payment slip. You can pay the government fee directly via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or any banking app.
  • Step 3 – Mandatory Biometric Verification: Under Punjab Excise regulations, live biometric verification is compulsory for both seller and buyer. We guide you through smooth biometric verification in Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi without long waiting queues.
  • Step 4 – Smart Card Issuance & Tracking: Once biometrics and fees are verified, your vehicle ownership updates in official Punjab Excise records and your new Smart Card is dispatched.

3. Transparent Fee Structure (Government Fee + Dastak Facilitation)

  • Government Transfer & Token Tax Fee: Varies based on vehicle engine capacity (CC), model year, and FBR filer/non-filer status. Use our interactive Punjab Vehicle Fee Calculator on `www.dastak90807.com` for an instant exact calculation.
  • Dastak Doorstep Facilitation Charge: A flat, transparent facilitation fee of PKR 950 applies for complete doorstep document handling, portal entry, and PSID generation under Code 90807.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (Vehicle Transfer & Token Tax)

  • Q: Can I transfer a vehicle in Punjab if the seller's biometric is not available?

A: No, Punjab Excise strictly requires live biometric verification from both the seller and buyer (or an official succession/power of attorney in special legal cases).

  • Q: How can I pay pending annual token tax from home in Gujar Khan or Rawalpindi?

A: Contact Facilitator Code 90807 on WhatsApp (0345-5583530) with your vehicle registration number. We generate your official Excise PSID slip within minutes for instant mobile banking payment.
