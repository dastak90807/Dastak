Guides FAQs

Are these guides from the government?

No. These guides are written by Muhammad Usman, an independent Registered Facilitator (Code 90807) under the Maryam Ki Dastak program. Fees and rules come from public Punjab Government sources. Always confirm the exact fee on the official PSID or the Punjab Government portal (dastak.punjab.gov.pk) before you pay.

How often are the guides updated?

Each guide shows its published date. I update a guide when a rule, fee or deadline changes. If you see something that looks out of date, message me on WhatsApp at +92-345-5583530.

Can I get help at my doorstep in Gujar Khan?

Yes. I help at your doorstep in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta, Daultala, Jatli, Bewal, Sukho and Jabbar. In the Dastak app choose Doorstep Delivery, select Self or On Behalf, and enter Facilitator Code 90807, or message me on WhatsApp.

Can I estimate my fee before I apply?

Yes. Use the free Dastak fee calculators for driving license, car token tax and transfer, and motorcycle registration and transfer. They give an estimate only. The official fee is generated by the Punjab system.
