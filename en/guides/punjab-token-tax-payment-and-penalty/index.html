PUNJAB VEHICLE & TOKEN TAX GUIDE

Punjab Token Tax 2026: Deadline, Penalty & How to Pay (Pakistan)

By Muhammad Usman · Facilitator Code 90807 · Published 24 November 2026

Quick answer

News reports dated 19 November 2026 say the Punjab Excise, Taxation and Narcotics Control Department set 30 November 2026 as the last date to pay outstanding vehicle token tax without extra penalties, with penalties starting on 1 December 2026. We checked the official Excise website on 24 November 2026 and did not find this notice there, so confirm your exact dues and dates in the official Dastak or ePay Punjab system, or on the Excise helpline 1035, before you pay.

What has been reported about the deadline?

  • Last date to pay outstanding token tax without extra penalties: 30 November 2026.
  • From 1 December 2026, reports say a 50% penalty applies to unpaid token tax of the previous financial year and a 25% penalty to unpaid tax of the current financial year.
  • Reports also mention possible action against defaulting vehicles, including cancellation of registration.

These figures come from news reports that cite a public notice of the Excise Department. Rules and penalty rates have changed during 2026, and earlier reports mentioned a higher penalty after a 30 September deadline. Always confirm the current amount for your vehicle in the official system before paying.

How much is my token tax?

Token tax depends on your vehicle type, engine capacity and financial year. The Excise Department publishes its rates and a motor vehicle tax calculator on its website, and the official system (Dastak or ePay Punjab) shows the amount payable for your vehicle. We do not list tax amounts on this page, because rates were revised for 2026-27 and the official amount is the one that counts. Our Punjab vehicle fee calculators give an estimate only.

How is token tax paid with a PSID?

Government fees are paid against a PSID (Payment Slip ID), a unique 17-digit number generated by the official government system for your payment. You do not pay the government amount to a person. You pay it against the PSID.

  1. With a Dastak facilitator: the registered facilitator submits your request in the official Dastak system. The system generates the PSID with the official amount, and you pay that PSID.
  2. Yourself through ePay Punjab: generate the PSID in the ePay Punjab app or website, then pay it.
  3. Paying a PSID: use mobile banking, internet banking, an ATM or a bank branch. Check that the amount and the department name match before you confirm.
  4. Keep the receipt after payment.

Screens and steps can change, so follow the prompts in the official system. Never pay through a link or account number sent by an unknown person or page.

Is the 10% rebate still available?

The Excise Department's rule gives a rebate of 10% of the annual token tax if the tax for the whole year is paid on or before 31 August of the financial year. For 2026-27 that date has passed, so do not rely on the rebate now.

Frequently asked questions

What is the reported last date to pay Punjab token tax without penalty?

News reports citing an Excise Department notice give 30 November 2026. Confirm in the official Dastak or ePay Punjab system, or on helpline 1035.

What penalties are reported from 1 December 2026?

Reports mention 50% on unpaid token tax of the previous financial year and 25% on the current year. The exact amount for your vehicle is shown in the official platform.

How is Punjab token tax paid?

Against an official PSID (Payment Slip ID). A Dastak facilitator generates it in the official Dastak system, or you can generate it yourself in ePay Punjab. Pay it through mobile banking, internet banking, an ATM or a bank branch and keep the receipt.

Where can I check the official token tax rates?

On the Excise, Taxation and Narcotics Control Department website, which lists token tax rates and a motor vehicle tax calculator.
