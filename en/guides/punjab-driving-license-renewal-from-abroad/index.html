Can Overseas Pakistanis Renew a Punjab License from Abroad?

Yes, overseas Pakistanis can renew a Punjab driving license from abroad when the licence is eligible for online renewal. The application is submitted through the official Punjab Dastak service, the government fee is paid by PSID, and the new card is delivered to an address in Punjab, usually a family member’s home.

Every week I help Pakistanis in Dubai, Riyadh, London, Milan, Toronto and other cities who need a valid Punjab licence for a visit home, a foreign licence conversion, or an international driving permit. Most of them assume they must fly back. In many cases they do not.

There is one firm limit. If your licence expired more than five years ago, it cannot be renewed from abroad. You must visit a licensing centre in Punjab in person. Very old licences, some categories and lost-licence cases may also need extra steps, so a quick document check on WhatsApp saves time.

Documents Needed to Renew a Punjab License from Abroad

To renew a Punjab driving license from abroad you need a valid CNIC, your old driving license, a recent photo on a white background, and a medical certificate if you are over 50. These requirements come from the official Dastak training manual and apply to overseas and local applicants alike.

  • CNIC (valid): your Pakistani identity card must not be expired. Renew it first if needed.
  • Old driving license: the original card or a clear scan, even if it has already expired.
  • Photo: shoulders up, plain white background, no glasses or caps.
  • Medical certificate: only if you are over 50 years of age.
  • FIR or loss report: only if the original licence is lost. See our duplicate driving license service.
  • Punjab delivery address and contact: a family member who can receive the card.

Send clear photos of these documents on WhatsApp before booking. You can also prepare everything with our document checklist.

Medical Certificate Rules for Overseas Applicants Over 50

The official Dastak training manual requires a medical certificate for Punjab driving license renewal when the applicant is over 50. Overseas applicants must include it with the other documents. If your medical check was done by a doctor abroad, confirm on WhatsApp first whether it can be used for your application.

This rule exists for road safety, and missing it is one of the most common reasons an older applicant’s renewal stops. Keep the certificate recent, clearly readable, and signed and stamped by the doctor.

If you are over 50 and abroad, message WhatsApp +92-345-5583530 with your age and where the certificate was issued. I will tell you the workable option for your case before you book.

The Punjab driving license renewal fee from abroad is the same government fee charged inside Pakistan. It depends on licence category, renewal period and any late penalty. e-License Download and Card Delivery to Family in Punjab

After approval, a Punjab driving license holder can check the digital e-license on the official Punjab licensing service using the CNIC number, while the physical card is couriered to the Punjab address on the application. Overseas applicants usually ask a family member to receive the card and send it abroad.

Expired, Lost or International License: Which Service Fits?

Overseas applicants should match their case to the right Punjab service: regular renewal for an expired or expiring licence, duplicate license for a lost or damaged card, and international driving permit renewal for driving abroad. Choosing the wrong service is a common reason applications are returned.

Common Mistakes That Delay Overseas License Renewals

The mistakes that most often delay a Punjab driving license renewal from abroad are an expired CNIC, a photo without a white background, a missing medical certificate for applicants over 50, choosing the wrong service, and giving a Punjab contact number that is switched off when the courier calls.

  1. Expired CNIC: renew your identity card before applying.
  2. Wrong photo: use a plain white background and a clear, recent photo.
  3. No medical certificate over 50: attach it with your documents.
  4. Licence expired over 5 years: this needs an in-person centre visit, not online renewal.
  5. Wrong service: a lost licence needs a duplicate, not a renewal.
  6. Unreachable receiver: keep the family member’s phone on until the card arrives.
