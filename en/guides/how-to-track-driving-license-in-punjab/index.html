PUNJAB DRIVING LICENSE GUIDE

How to Track Your Driving License in Punjab: Complete Step-by-Step Guide (DLIMS & Pakistan Post)

By Muhammad Usman · Registered Facilitator · Published 23 November 2026

Quick answer

Tracking a Punjab driving license has two stages: check approval, printing and dispatch status on the official DLIMS tracking page using your CNIC, then follow the tracking ID on the Pakistan Post (UMS) portal to see postal delivery progress.

Waiting for your physical smart card after applying for a new driving license, renewal, or duplicate copy in Punjab? Knowing how to track your driving license in Punjab saves unnecessary visits to licensing centers and lets you check printing and courier dispatch status from home.

Tracking works in two simple stages: first checking application, printing and dispatch status on the official DLIMS Punjab portal using your CNIC, and second following the physical parcel through Pakistan Post (UMS).

Stage 1: Check Printing & Dispatch Status on DLIMS Punjab (By CNIC)

Once your application, any required verification and fee payment are completed through a licensing center, Khidmat Markaz or the official Dastak service, your file moves through verification, printing, and postal dispatch. To check your status:

  1. Open the official DLIMS tracking page: use the DLIMS tracking page, or open the same verified link from our Citizen Tools directory.
  2. Enter your CNIC number: type your 13-digit CNIC without dashes, or enter whatever details the page asks for.
  3. Complete the security check and submit: follow the on-screen prompts to view your status.
  4. Read your status: wording can change, but you will typically see one of these stages:
    • In process / under verification: your application data is still being checked by the licensing authority.
    • Approved / ready for printing: your license has been approved and is waiting for card printing.
    • Printed: your card has been printed and is waiting to be handed over for dispatch.
    • Dispatched: your card has been handed to the postal service. If a tracking or consignment ID is shown, copy it.

Stage 2: Track Physical Card Delivery via Pakistan Post (UMS / EMTTS)

Many applicants see Dispatched on DLIMS and wonder when the card will arrive. Once a tracking ID is available, your smart card is sent to your registered address through Pakistan Post.

  1. Copy your tracking ID: save the tracking or consignment number shown on your DLIMS result.
  2. Open the Pakistan Post portal: go to the Pakistan Post EMTTS / UMS portal, also linked in our Citizen Tools section.
  3. Find the tracking option: look for the Tracking ID or Track & Trace link on the portal and enter your number there. The page layout may change, so follow the portal's own prompts.
  4. Read the updates: where available, the result shows postal transit progress and arrival at your local delivery post office.
Helpful tip: keep your registered mobile number active and reachable. Delivery staff may try to contact you before delivering your card.

Need to Drive Before Your Physical Card Arrives? Check Your Punjab e-License

If your DLIMS status shows Approved, Printed, or Dispatched, check whether an e-License is available for your record:

  • Open the official DLIMS e-License page (also on our Citizen Tools page).
  • Enter the details requested, usually your CNIC and other identifying information.
  • If a digital e-License is available, download the PDF and keep a copy on your phone.
  • Carry your original CNIC as well. Acceptance in a given situation is decided by the traffic authorities, so confirm with your licensing office if you are unsure.

Common Reasons for Driving License Card Delivery Delays

  • Incomplete or outdated address: cards are sent to the address in your licensing record. If it is incomplete, visit your area's delivery post office with your tracking ID and original CNIC to ask for help.
  • High printing volume: during busy periods, printing and dispatch can take longer than usual, sometimes several weeks.
  • Unreachable phone number: if delivery staff cannot reach you, the parcel may be held at the local post office for some time before being returned.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How many days does it take to receive a driving license in Punjab?

Many applicants receive their smart card in roughly two to four weeks after verification and fee submission, but timing varies with printing volume and postal delivery. The licensing authority controls printing and dispatch, so use the DLIMS tracking page for your latest status.

How can I check my Punjab driving license tracking without a token number?

The DLIMS tracking page normally asks for your CNIC number, though it may request additional details. If your card has been dispatched, the result may show a postal tracking or consignment ID. Follow the prompts on the official page.

What should I do if DLIMS shows Dispatched but I have not received my card?

Note the tracking or consignment ID shown on DLIMS and check it through the Pakistan Post UMS portal. If it shows arrival at your local post office, visit with your original CNIC and tracking number to ask about collection.

Is the Punjab e-License valid while waiting for the physical smart card?

If DLIMS offers an e-License for your record, you can download it as a PDF and keep it on your phone. Whether it is accepted in a particular situation is decided by the traffic authorities, so carry your original CNIC and confirm with your licensing office if unsure.
