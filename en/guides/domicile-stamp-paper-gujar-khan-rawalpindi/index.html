GUJAR KHAN & RAWALPINDI DOCUMENT GUIDE

Domicile & e-Stamp Paper in Gujar Khan and Rawalpindi: Application Guide

By Muhammad Usman · Facilitator Code 90807 · Updated 17 November 2026

Quick answer

For a Punjab domicile application, confirm your district’s eligibility and document checklist, prepare identity and residence records, and submit through the relevant official service. For an e-stamp, use the Punjab e-stamping system for the document purpose and verify the issued record. Muhammad Usman, Facilitator Code 90807, provides preparation and available doorstep assistance in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi and surrounding areas.

What should I prepare for a domicile application?

  • Your CNIC or B-Form, as applicable.
  • Parent or spouse identity and relationship documents where required.
  • Proof of residence requested by the relevant office.
  • Educational records and a recent photograph if requested.
  • The required application form and affidavit, where applicable.

The checklist depends on your age, district and application type. Confirm the requirements before paying for affidavits or preparing a new file.

How do I apply for a domicile certificate?

  1. Check your case with the relevant domicile office or available Dastak service.
  2. Prepare identity, residence and other requested records.
  3. Submit the official application and pay the required government fee.
  4. Retain your application reference and respond to any verification request.
  5. Receive the document after approval by the competent authority.

For an existing record, ask whether you need a duplicate, correction, or transfer NOC instead of a fresh application. See our domicile service page.

What can an e-stamp be used for?

An e-stamp may be required for an affidavit, rent agreement, undertaking or another document. The correct instrument, wording and duty depend on the transaction. Confirm the receiving authority’s requirements before generating the challan.

Use the official Punjab e-stamping portal for the available official process. Facilitation covers document preparation and portal guidance; the official system generates the record.

How can I verify domicile and e-stamp records?

Use the official domicile and e-stamp verification links on Citizen Tools & Portals. Follow the identifiers requested by each portal and compare the returned record with your document. Ask the issuing department about missing or mismatched records.

Frequently asked questions

Is a domicile certificate the same as an e-stamp affidavit?

No. A domicile certificate is issued by the competent authority. An affidavit on e-stamp paper may be a supporting document where required.

Can the facilitator issue my domicile certificate?

The facilitator helps with preparation and the available official application process. The competent department approves and issues the certificate.

Where can I verify an e-stamp?

Use the official Punjab e-stamping verification service and provide the identifier requested on the portal.
