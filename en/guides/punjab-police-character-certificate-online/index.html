PUNJAB POLICE DOCUMENT GUIDE

Punjab Police Character Certificate: Documents & Steps (Pakistan)

By Muhammad Usman · Facilitator Code 90807 · Updated 24 November 2026

Quick answer

For a Punjab Police character certificate, first confirm the Punjab Police Khidmat Markaz requirements for your purpose, prepare your identity and supporting documents, and apply through the available official channel. Muhammad Usman (Dastak Facilitator Code 90807) helps applicants across Punjab: file review and guidance online by WhatsApp, and in-person help by arrangement in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi and nearby areas. Punjab Police handles verification and issuance.

When might I need a police character certificate?

An employer, visa office, educational institution or immigration authority may request a police character certificate. Confirm exactly which certificate and coverage period the receiving organisation requires. A Police Character Certificate and General Police Verification are distinct services.

Which documents should I prepare?

  • Your original CNIC and readable copies.
  • Your passport details or copy where required for travel or overseas use.
  • A recent photograph if requested.
  • Your present and previous address details for the requested period.
  • Any purpose-specific or representative documents requested by Punjab Police.

Use the Police Khidmat Markaz website to check the current checklist. Requirements for overseas applicants and representatives can differ.

How do I request help with my certificate?

  1. Call or WhatsApp 0345-5583530 with the service and your district (anywhere in Punjab).
  2. Confirm the document checklist and how your application will be submitted.
  3. Have the registered facilitator review the file and assist with the available official submission steps.
  4. Keep the official application reference and payment receipt.
  5. Follow the department’s verification, approval and collection or delivery instructions.

How can I verify an issued character certificate?

Use the official Punjab Police certificate verification tool. Enter the information requested on that page. Contact the issuing office if the record or certificate details do not match.

Which areas do you cover?

Online guidance and file checks are available for applicants across Punjab. In-person visits are by arrangement in Gujar Khan, Jatli, Bewal, Daultala, Mandra, Sukho, Qazian, Kallar Syedan, Kahuta and Rawalpindi. Availability depends on your address and the applicable official service.

Frequently asked questions

Is a character certificate the same as general police verification?

They are separate services. Ask the organisation requesting your document which service it needs before applying.

Do you help with police character certificates outside Gujar Khan?

Yes. Dastak 90807 helps applicants across Punjab, Pakistan with document checks and guidance online by WhatsApp. In-person visits are by arrangement in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi and nearby areas.

Can I apply from overseas?

Check the Punjab Police requirements for overseas applicants and authorised representatives. Ask for a document pre-check before arranging a submission.

Does a facilitator approve my character certificate?

No. The facilitator helps prepare and submit the application; Punjab Police verifies the record and decides issuance.
