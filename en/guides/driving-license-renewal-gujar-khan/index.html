GUJAR KHAN & RAWALPINDI DOCUMENT GUIDE

Online Driving Licence Renewal in Gujar Khan & Rawalpindi

By Muhammad Usman · Facilitator Code 90807 · Updated 17 November 2026

Quick answer

Start with the official Punjab DLIMS/Dastak service, check eligibility for your licence category, prepare your identity and existing licence documents, and follow the application and PSID payment steps. What should I prepare for a Punjab driving licence renewal?

  • Your original CNIC and clear copies of both sides.
  • Your existing or expired driving licence and its details.
  • A recent, clear photograph.
  • A medical fitness certificate where the portal requires it for your age or category.
  • An active mobile number and access to your application record.

Confirm the service-specific checklist in the official portal before your visit. Requirements can differ by licence category and application history.

How does online renewal work?

  1. Open Punjab DLIMS and follow its current Dastak service route.
  2. Select the relevant renewal service and check your eligibility.
  3. Enter the licence details and upload the requested documents.
  4. Generate the official PSID and pay through an accepted channel.
  5. Keep the receipt and application reference, then follow the portal’s status and delivery instructions.

Some categories or older expired licences may require an in-person licensing-centre visit. Confirm eligibility before arranging doorstep assistance.

How much does renewal cost?

The estimate depends on your vehicle category, validity period, expiry date, applicable late fees, and delivery charges. Use our driving licence fee calculator for a breakdown. The official PSID determines the government amount payable.

How do I track my licence or download an e-licence?

Use the DLIMS tracking, verification, and e-licence tools listed on our Citizen Tools & Portals page. Follow the official page’s prompts and keep your application reference available. Downloaded records and physical card delivery are separate steps.

Frequently asked questions

Can Muhammad Usman help renew my licence from home?

Muhammad Usman provides document checks and application assistance for available services in Gujar Khan, Rawalpindi and nearby areas. Confirm your licence category and eligibility before booking.

Is an online fee estimate the final payable fee?

The licensing authority controls approval and dispatch. Ask for the current estimate when your application is submitted.
